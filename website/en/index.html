Five ways to use it

The shortcuts are defaults; record your own in Settings.

Translate the selection

⌥ A

The panel appears with your selection and the translation streams in. Your language goes into your usual foreign language and anything else comes into yours; they default to Simplified Chinese and English, and take any language, dialect or register.

Polish your writing

Tab ⏎

In the panel, press Tab to switch to improving, then Return. The improved text stays in its own language, ready to paste back.

Translate text on screen

⌥ S

For images, videos and interfaces you cannot select, frame what you want translated. Cida recognizes the text on your Mac, then sends it to your model.

Read one paragraph in place

⌥ D

Point at a paragraph and press the shortcut. It turns into its translation where it stands, among the original text around it; press again to turn it back.

Translate a whole window

⌥ ⇧ D

For a Slack channel or a long article in a language you do not read. New text is translated as it appears and a second press stops; code stays as it is, and clicks and scrolling still reach the app.

Set up in three steps

  1. Open Cida.dmg

    Drag 辞达 into Applications and open it. The installer is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple; Cida lives in the menu bar, not the Dock.

  2. Let an AI assistant set up the model

    Press ⌘ ,, click 复制配置提示词 to copy the configuration prompt, and give it to Claude Code, Codex or another assistant. It asks which service you use, writes the configuration and checks that the model answers.

  3. Store the API key in the Keychain

    The key never passes through the assistant: it asks you to copy the key and run one command that stores it in the Keychain. A model running on your Mac needs no key.

Your text goes only where you send it

  • The API key is stored only in the macOS Keychain.
  • There is no history and no telemetry; translation and improvement requests go only to the provider you chose.
  • Screenshots are recognized on your Mac with Apple’s Vision and never leave it. In-place translation reads only the text apps expose and never captures the screen.

What’s new

Cida checks for updates once a day. When a new version is out, its panel shows what changed, written in Chinese, and installs the update once you agree.
